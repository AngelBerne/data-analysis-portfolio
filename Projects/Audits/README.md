






## Situation:
When I started working in Asset Protection, I noticed that tracking our goals was difficult and sometimes inaccurate. We depended on a Tableau table that was updated by a third party, so we didn't have control over when the data was refreshed.
One of my coworkers had developed a workaround where he exported the raw data into Excel and manually sorted and organized it. Although that worked, the process was dependent on one person. Only a few people knew how to do it, so if that person was unavailable, the team had difficulty getting an accurate view of our results.

## Task:
I wanted to find a more reliable and repeatable way to work with the raw data. My goal was to create an Excel file that could automate the process and allow the team to see both the overall goal and individual goals in one place.

## Action:
First, I spoke with my supervisor and requested access to the raw data. Then I worked with my coworker to understand the existing process. I asked him which columns he used, how he sorted the information, and what steps he followed to make sure the data was accurate.
After understanding the process, I realized that I could use Power Query to automate those steps. I created a repeatable process that transformed and organized the raw data automatically instead of requiring someone to manually prepare it every time.
I presented the solution to my supervisor, and he gave me additional feedback. He suggested that we track the results by week and include individual goals by position. I incorporated those changes into the file.

## Result:
The new process reduced the time spent reviewing and preparing the numbers during each meeting by about 20 minutes. What previously took around 20 minutes could now be completed in about 5 minutes with only a few clicks.
It also helped make the goals more balanced and consistent across all categories, because the team could clearly see which areas were performing well and which ones needed more attention.
The biggest impact was on the individual goals. Since the information was available in one place and could be updated consistently, everyone on the team could see where each category needed more focus and understand how their individual performance contributed to the overall goal.
This allowed the team to spend less time preparing numbers and more time taking action based on the data.
